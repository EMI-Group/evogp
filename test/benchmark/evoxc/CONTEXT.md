# `test/benchmark/evoxc/` — the evoxc engine vs plain-PyTorch controlled harness

## Intent
One question, answered with records: what does the evoxc-based EvoGP engine cost per evaluation compared with the plain-PyTorch implementation of the same tree evaluation, on identical populations, in the same session, warm/steady-state.
The harness covers FOUR evoxc routes — the shipped `auto` policy over `fused`/`dense`/`specialized`, the explicit `fused` (Triton) request and the opt-in `batch` merge — and `auto` now reaches the `fused` route on a device box: ONE Triton kernel launch for the whole population whenever triton is importable, a torch device is usable and `pop_size >= EVOGP_FUSED_MIN_POP` (16), falling back only otherwise to its dense+iree (pop ≥ `EVOGP_DENSE_AUTO_MIN_POP` 64) / specialized+numpy answers.
The opt-in `batch` route is the only one `auto` never resolves.
The answers live in seven reports of record — `FINAL.md` (the shipped `specialized`/`dense` routes against both torch baselines), `BATCH.md` (the opt-in `batch` route plus its three optimization rounds), `LARGE_SCALE.md` (the large-scale column at pop 2 048–8 192 / `N` 4 096–16 384), `LARGE_SCALE_ROUND2.md` (that column re-measured after the `EVOGP_BATCH_DISPATCH_FORMATION`, corrected-`auto` and artifact-tier work), `results/t1a1_auto_r3/README.md` (the SHIPPED-default campaign of record, `auto` → fused, read against `results/t1a1_fused/README.md` as the explicit-fused column and `results/t1a1_baseline/BASELINE.md` as the pre-fused one), `results/t1a1_auto/README.md` (the shipped default's one-screen verdict page, which quotes `t1a1_auto_r3/` and keeps its own pre-change JSON as the A/B baseline) and `results/t1a1_fused/README.md` (the EXPLICIT `EVOGP_EVAL_MODE=fused` column, read against `results/t1a1_baseline/` as the pre-fused baseline).
Every arm is validated numerically against `../../src/evogp/tests/reference_eval.py` before its timings are trusted (`VALIDATION.md`), and every record states its environment, the route that actually resolved and the cold compile it paid.
A batch-route record also splits one evaluation into ten stages (`stages.py`) — host decode/dedupe/trace/merge, then lower/compile/load, then upload/run/gather — names the merge's compile options (`band_report.dispatch_formation`) and, on a disk-tier arm, whether the persistent artifact directory served the call (`artifact.py`).
The historical siblings `../r2/`, `../r4/`, `../r5/`, `../r6/` and `../REPORT.md` compare the same engine with the **deleted legacy CUDA engine** at `b613503`; they are frozen history and are never edited from here.

## Reports of record
- `FINAL.md` — the shipped routes: arm redefinition, headline/before-after/decomposition tables, cold-start economics, 50- and 200-generation training runs, noise audit, gate, reproduce block.
- `BATCH.md` — the opt-in `batch` route: route description, `EVOGP_BATCH_*` knob table, warm/`gp_step`/compile-economics tables, the one winning regime, numeric validation, gate, reproduce commands; §13 round 1 (chunked compile, plan key, per-stage split), §14 round 2 (band policy), §15 round 3 (per-cell warm/cold/loop table, compile-pool break-even, band-size policy).
- `LARGE_SCALE.md` — round 1 of the large-scale column: the ten-cell grid and what was trimmed, per-cell warm/cold/`gp_step` tables, the batch-vs-dense verdict, the ten-stage warm/cold split, the quadratic compile law with its two declared-infeasible cells, the loop ratios, the band-size and pool-width sweeps, the `auto` rows-ceiling and dense-OOM findings. Its `batch` and `evoxc-auto` columns are superseded by `LARGE_SCALE_ROUND2.md`; its `dense` and raised-dense columns were re-measured there and agree within noise, while its `torch-per-tree` column reads 1.2–9.3 % higher in the re-measured window (an instrument-side drift, uniform in sign).
- `LARGE_SCALE_ROUND2.md` — the round after three landed improvements: the dispatch-formation knob (default `auto` ⇒ IREE aggressive formation on a rocm/cuda merged plan), the corrected `auto` (rows rule turned into a memory envelope plus a power-of-two trace-cap raise) and the batch route's persistent artifact tier. §1 column, §2 warm/cold/ratio tables with BEFORE/AFTER columns, §2.5 what the re-measured window moved, §3 the formation knob, §4 the corrected `auto`, §5 `gp_step`/`forward` plus the whole-column value paths, §6 the artifact tier, §7 pool width and band size, §8 the pop-8 192 cell, §9 verdict, §10 gaps and owners, §11 evidence layout + reproduce + gate.
  `main/` was re-measured with all three value workloads (`sr_fitness`, `forward`, `batch_forward`) for 8 cells × 7 arms, the `d7` cell without the OOM-prone plain `evoxc-dense-iree` arm: 55 records at one tree root, gate 1 321 checks / 0 failures (+16 declared WARN), and the `sr_fitness` column replicated the superseded single-workload window (45 of 54 comparable values inside ±2 %; the drift sits in the torch-per-tree column and the formation control, whose `p2048` value reads 14.00 ms against 12.81).
  Its verdict in one line: above ~4 000 rows and past the dense trace cap the warm order is **batch-first** (7 of 8 `main/` cells against default dense, 1.10–6.20× cheaper, 5.21× against a raised-cap dense on `d7`; the only loss is `p2048-L64-i32-N1024` at 1.12×, and `p8192` adds an eighth win at 1.81×) while `torch-batched` still beats every compiled route on 8 of 9 cells, a real generation loop still loses by 68–178×, and the compile column (three plans per cell now: 8.7 s at pop 256 up to 261 s at pop 4 096 / `d7`) plus the per-generation replan stay the disqualifier.
  The per-cell formation delta is window-sensitive and only its sign and the ordering are robust: `p2048-L64-i32-N1024` reads −11.4 % in the re-measured window against −4.9 % in the 3-cell A/B, with the control arm's own cold spread (50.7 s vs 43.8 s) as the visible cause.
- `results/t1a1_auto_r3/README.md` — the SHIPPED-DEFAULT campaign of record (`auto`, i.e. the fused route on this box, measured on the release tree `919f7af`): six cells × `evoxc-auto` / `torch-batched` over `sr_fitness` + `batch_forward` + `forward` + `gp_step`, read against `results/t1a1_fused/README.md` as the explicit-fused column and `results/t1a1_baseline/` as the pre-fused default; gate 12 records / 356 checks / 0 failures (+6 declared digest WARN). Verdict: warm `batch_forward` reads **0.0449–0.1152 ms = 80.2×–228.9× `torch-batched`**, `sr_fitness` **0.0572–0.1262 ms (60.2×–208.8×)**, `forward` **0.0390–0.0722 ms** and a whole GP generation **2.841–3.282 ms** with evaluation at 0.433–0.471 ms (**14.2–15.4 %**); every `evoxc-auto` record resolves `eval_mode auto` / `engine fused` / `backend iree` with `served_every_workload true`, 24 `fused_kernel_launches` (21 of them bound launches) and 0 declines.
- `results/t1a1_auto/README.md` — the shipped default's one-screen verdict page, which quotes `results/t1a1_auto_r3/`; its own JSON are the PRE-host-glue-round evidence (`5a699ca`) and stay the A/B baseline: six cells × `evoxc-auto` / `torch-batched` / `torch-per-tree` / `evoxc-dense-iree` over `sr_fitness` + `batch_forward` + `forward` + `gp_step`, gate 23 records / 640 checks / 0 failures (+7 WARN: 1 allowed missing arm and 6 digest splits). `results/t1a1_auto_r2/` is the previous round's window (12 records / 356 checks / 0 failures, +6 digest WARN), superseded on every number by `t1a1_auto_r3/` and kept beside `t1a1_auto/` as the second A/B baseline.
- `results/t1a1_fused/README.md` — the EXPLICIT `fused` (Triton) route's campaign (`EVOGP_EVAL_MODE=fused`): six cells × `evoxc-fused-iree` plus `torch-batched` (and `evoxc-dense-iree` on four, `evoxc-dense-iree-raised` on `d7`, `evoxc-batch-iree` on three) over `sr_fitness` + `batch_forward` + `forward` + `gp_step`, read against `results/t1a1_baseline/` as the pre-fused baseline; gate 20 records / 580 checks / 0 failures. Verdict: **41×–157× over `torch-batched` on `batch_forward`** (32×–131× on `sr_fitness`) with bitwise value paths.
  `src/evogp/tree/FUSED.md` — the fused engine's own design/report document (route description, kernel geometry, the memory/population envelope beside its `ENVELOPE.md`, verdicts); its evidence now lives here in `results/t1a1_fused/`, not in `src/`.
- `results/t1a1_baseline/BASELINE.md` — the reference baseline for the joint optimization effort and the PRE-FUSED default: six cells × five arms (the `d7` cell without the OOM-prone plain `evoxc-dense-iree`) on `batch_forward` + `sr_fitness`, whose `evoxc-auto-iree` resolved dense+iree and which scheduled neither `forward` nor `gp_step`; with its four-cell dense `decompose/` column and three oracle `validation/` records; `main/` is the gateable campaign directory (29 records / 538 checks / 6 declared scoped-schedule failures).
- `VALIDATION.md` — the oracle validation of the final sweep and the cross-arm digest-agreement finding; `SUMMARY.md` — the rendered earlier baseline round (its `evoxc-auto` column predates the dense policy); `README.md` — methodology, arms, grid, the campaign commands of record.

## Contents — harness modules
- `common.py` — the one definition of every workload: the cell grid (`main_cells()`/`n64_cells()`/`long_tree_cells()`/`large_cells()`/`parse_cell`; the large-scale column is ten cells over four axes — population scale 2048/4096/8192, row scale 4096/16384 at pop 256 and 1000, the node-count cells `p2048-L128-i32-N1024-d7` / `p2048-L256-i32-N1024-d8` above the dense trace cap, one `-i64` input-width cell), deterministic `build_workload`, `env_block`, `time_call` (cold call split from warmup+reps medians), `digest`, `population_signature`, `src_root()`/`checkout_root()`.
  A cell is `cell(pop, tree_len, input_len, rows, max_layer_cnt=5)` and its id gains a `-d<k>` suffix only when that depth cap is not 5, so every pre-existing cell id stays byte-identical.
  The memory rule is `cell_tensor_bytes(cell)` = `pop * tree_len * rows * 4` bytes (the dense route's largest intermediate) against `CELL_TENSOR_CEILING_BYTES` = 8 GiB on the 191.7 GB-HBM box; the worst large-scale cell is 4.19 GB.
  It single-sources the route knobs: `BATCH_KNOBS`/`BATCH_ENV_VARS` (dispatch formation included), `DENSE_ENV_VARS` (the trace cap plus `auto`'s `EVOGP_DENSE_AUTO_BUDGET_MB` and its rows pin `EVOGP_DENSE_AUTO_MAX_ROWS`), `ARTIFACT_ENV_VARS` (the dense AND batch disk tier's one `EVOGP_EVOXC_ARTIFACT_DIR`), `FUSED_ENV_VARS` (the fused route's `EVOGP_FUSED_MIN_POP`/`_BLOCK_ROWS`/`_NUM_WARPS`/`_MAX_SCRATCH_MB`/`_HOST_MEMO`) and `ROUTE_ENV_VARS` (every var an arm clears before applying its own spec), plus `is_batch_arm`, `batch_engine_present`, `batch_policy`, the disk tier's directory hook (`ARTIFACT_DIR_ENV` = `EVOGP_BENCH_ARTIFACT_DIR`, `DEFAULT_ARTIFACT_DIR`, `ARTIFACT_DIR_PLACEHOLDER` = `{artifact_dir}`, `artifact_dir()`) and `arm_knob_env(arm)`.
  `route_decision` asks the policy's own `resolve_eval_mode`/`resolve_backend_name`/`should_use_dense`/`auto_rows_decline`, passing the population SHAPE (`pop_size=`/`rows=`/`tree_len=`), and dispatches to `batch_route_decision` for `EVOGP_EVAL_MODE=batch`, so a record reports the real decision.
  It asks the ONE resolver (`dense_engine.resolve_route`) whenever the requested eval mode resolves to `fused` OR to `auto` with no explicit backend (`_explicit_backend`, env fallback included) and records `engine`/`backend`/`would_use_fused`/`fused_fallback_backend`/`fused_reason`, so an `evoxc-auto` record names the engine that served it — the Triton kernel or the route's fall-back to `specialized`; a `resolve_engine=False` caller (the plain-torch arms) records no engine, because it ran none.
- `band_report.py` — the one owner of the band/merge vocabulary of one merged plan (torch-free, evogp-free): `BAND_REPORT_KEYS`, `BAND_COST_NOTE`/`BAND_ABSENT_NOTE`, `band_report(...)` (getattr-probed reader of `describe_plan` and the plan's own report), and the compile-option seam `dispatch_formation` (the EFFECTIVE spelling, hence in every memo/executable/artifact key) next to `dispatch_formation_requested`; both `null` on a description that predates the knob, and `cost_estimate`/`flat_cost_estimate` read `null` unless `EVOGP_BATCH_BANDS=auto` ran the cost model. `common.py` re-exports all four names.
- `counter_blocks.py` — the strict number/block helpers every optional batch counter surface shares: `number`/`integer`/`rounded`/`delta`/`delta_int`/`median`/`maximum` (a missing or non-numeric value reads `None`, never `0`), `has_any`, `accessor`, `block`, `keys_of`.
- `stages.py` — the one owner of the ten-stage vocabulary (`decode, dedup, trace, merge, lower, compile, load, upload, run, gather`), the `batch_<stage>_calls`/`_total_ms`/`_max_ms` keys plus `batch_compile_wall_ms`/`batch_compile_jobs`, the optional `batch_stage_stats()`/`batch_stage_reset()` contract and every derived block (`unavailable_block`, `snapshot_block`, `delta_block`, `median_block`, `StageStatsProbe`, `plan_fields`/`plan_block`, `stage_totals`, `summary_block`, `compile_share`).
  Null, never zero: an absent accessor, a missing key or a half-read window reads `None` (or `available: false`) plus a note; `batch_<stage>_max_ms` is the after snapshot's process-lifetime maximum, not a delta; `compile_share` may exceed 1 because its numerator sums the compile stage's per-chunk timers while the pool runs them concurrently.
- `artifact.py` — the batch route's on-disk executable tier vocabulary: `STATS_FN_PATH` (`evogp.tree.batch_engine.batch_artifact_stats`), the seven counters (`batch_artifact_enabled`/`_files` as STATE, `_hits`/`_misses`/`_load_failures`/`_stores`/`_save_failures` as window deltas), `stats_fn` and `unavailable_block`/`snapshot_block`/`delta_block`/`window_block` (with `attempts`/`hit_rate`); a tier that is present but OFF reads `available: true` with `batch_artifact_enabled` 0 plus `DISABLED_NOTE`.
- `content.py` — the content-addressed plan-key vocabulary: `STATS_FN_PATH` (`evogp.tree.batch_engine.batch_content_stats`), the five counters `batch_content_hits`/`_misses`/`_rejects`/`_entries`/`_maxsize`, `stats_fn`, and the derived blocks incl. `row_state`/`summary_block`; `hit_rate` is `None` when there was no attempt, and an absent accessor records `available: false` with nulls plus a note naming the missing function.
- `arms_torch.py` — the two plain-torch baselines: `PerTreeEvaluator` (Python tree walk) and `BatchedEvaluator` (one vectorized pass over the population); op semantics copied from the oracle.
- `arms.py` — the arm namespace: `TorchArm`/`EvoxcArm`, `resolve_knob_value` (`{tree_len}`/`{pop}`/`{input_len}`/`{rows}`/`{artifact_dir}` substitution; literal values unchanged; `ValueError` naming the arm and placeholder), `apply_arm_env(arm, cell=None)` (route selectors plus the spec's `knobs`, applied RESOLVED into `env.applied_knobs`; it clears every `common.ROUTE_ENV_VARS` entry first for EVERY arm so no route knob leaks between arms), `make_arm`, `SRLossProblem` + `make_pipeline` for `gp_step`.
  `evoxc-auto` leaves both knobs unset so it times the shipped policy (the fused route on a device box); `evoxc-auto-iree` pins the backend to iree, and an explicit backend never reaches the fused route under `auto`, so it times the dense+iree / specialized+iree answers. The batch arms are `evoxc-batch-iree` (no knob ⇒ `dispatch_formation=auto`), `-flat` (`EVOGP_BATCH_BANDS=flat`), `-aggressive`, `-default` (the PRE-CHANGE control of the merged compile) and `-artifact` (`EVOGP_EVOXC_ARTIFACT_DIR={artifact_dir}`), plus the pool pins `-artifact-p{1,2,8}` (they set `EVOGP_BATCH_COMPILE_WORKERS`, the only way a campaign can reach the pool width).
- `worker.py` — ONE process, ONE `(cell, arm)`: cold call, warmup, reps, cache deltas, value digests, `gp_step` split into eval/operator, per-workload `first_call_ms`; honors `--tree-root` and passes the cell dict into `apply_arm_env`.
  It records `dense_policy` (`auto_min_pop`, `auto_max_rows`, `auto_budget_mb`, `max_tree_len_cap`), `dense_route` (`probe_ms` from a direct `dense_engine.sr_fitness_dense` probe, so it says whether dense served the cell or the cap declined it), `auto_trace_cap` (the cap `auto` resolves per workload next to the base cap, the ceiling and `trace_cap_pinned()`), and for a batch arm `batch_policy`/`batch_cache`/`batch_plans`/`batch_plan_summary` plus the optional stage, content and artifact windows (`batch_cache.artifact_before_workloads`/`artifact_after`/`artifact_keys`/`artifact_totals`/`artifact_dir`).
  All null-never-zero; it refuses to write a record when the imported tree cannot run the route (clear `SystemExit`, campaign status `failed`).
  Any arm that MAY reach the fused route (`may_use_fused`: registry eval mode `fused`, `auto` or unset) additionally records the fused engine's own counter surface (`FUSED_STATS_KEYS`, 15 keys, `fused_engine.fused_cache_stats`): `cache.fused` is a before → after window delta over the 9 monotone counters with the 6 `FUSED_GAUGE_KEYS` (`fused_scratch_entries`/`_bytes`, `fused_block_rows`, `fused_num_warps`, …) read as AFTER values, plus the per-record `fused_cache` block (`stats_before_workloads`/`stats_after`/`totals`/`served_every_workload`) built by `fused_window_block`, so an `auto` record carries the same launch proof (`record["fused_cache"]`, per-workload `cache.fused*`) as an explicitly-fused one.
  `fused_served(block, calls)` is the LAUNCH PROOF: the fused route compiles no evoxc graph, so `cache.compile_misses_first_call` is 0 by construction and cannot distinguish a served call from a silent `FusedUnsupported` fallback — a positive `fused_kernel_launches` delta (or a cold-cache `fused_kernel_compiles`) is the only evidence, and it reads `None` rather than `False` when the counters are absent.
- `campaign.py` — the sequential driver: one subprocess per config, idle-device guard (`rocm-smi --showpids`, excluding the driver's own ancestor chain), `--tree-root`/`--evoxc-root`/`--resume`/scope policy, `--cells main|n64|long|large|<list>` (`CELL_COLUMNS` behind `select_cells`; any other token is one `common.parse_cell` id), `--arms all|torch|evoxc|batch|<list>`, `--workloads`, `--warmup`/`--reps`/`--gp-warmup`/`--gp-reps`/`--timeout`, per-config `.log` + `campaign.json` with the guard verdict per config and the batch arms' specs; `--dry-run` is torch-free and prints one `batch policy …`, one `arm knobs <arm> …` and one `dense knob <arm> <cell> …` line per selection.
  `CELL_EXCLUSIONS` is a `(cell id, arm)` table consulted unconditionally by `in_scope()` (`--full-specialized-iree` included) for a config the route cannot survive; its one entry excludes `evoxc-dense-iree` on `p2048-L128-i32-N1024-d7` (explicit dense past the trace cap declines into per-structure specialized+iree with unbounded artifact growth and OOM-kills the job unit), the reason is recorded in `campaign.json`'s `protocol.scope_dropped[*].reason` + `protocol.cell_exclusions`, and `--dry-run` prints it as `would drop <cell> <arm>: <reason>`.
  `--artifact-dir` (`resolve_artifact_dir`: CLI > `$EVOGP_BENCH_ARTIFACT_DIR` > `<out>/artifacts`) points the disk tier at ONE fixed path reused across processes, recorded as `protocol.artifact_dir`/`artifact_dir_env`/`artifact_note`.
- `test_large_cells.py` — the torch-free pytest check of the large-scale column (imports `common`/`campaign` only): pins the grid order, the id spelling and `parse_cell` round-trip, the `-d<k>`-only-above-cap-5 rule, the memory rule against `CELL_TENSOR_CEILING_BYTES`, coverage of the four axes and `campaign.select_cells("large")`.
- `test_arm_registry.py` — the torch-free sibling that pins the arm registry and the route-knob plumbing: the `BATCH_KNOBS` entries (dispatch formation included), `ARTIFACT_ENV_VARS`/`DENSE_ENV_VARS`/`FUSED_ENV_VARS`, the unchanged arm ids and order (the two fused arms last, after the six batch arms), the three formation/artifact arms' exact env, `--arms batch` selecting all batch arms, `common.artifact_dir()`'s fallback, that no arm leaks a route knob, the cell-template contract, `route_decision`'s shape passing (fake dense module), the fused route's `resolve_route` call and its `fused` `kind`, the band report's formation keys, the artifact block's null-vs-zero semantics, `worker.fused_served` being the only existence proof of a fused launch, `--dry-run` naming each variable exactly once, and `resolve_artifact_dir`'s precedence.
- `validate.py` — every arm vs the oracle and vs the engine, three function sets (`arith` bitwise-mandatory, `rich` transcendental spread, `ternary` for the `if` operand order), 12 cases over four scheduled cells; it has no batch entry of its own.
- `decompose.py` — per-call phase breakdown of one cell on both routes (inclusive `perf_counter_ns` timers on the engine's own functions, residual row for host glue, seam counters, cold compile separate).
- `summarize.py` — stdlib-only renderer: environment block, per-workload tables (median with min in parentheses), `auto` route per cell, speedups vs both torch arms, the `gp_step` section (total, eval/ops split, operator share, `best_fitness`), cold-compile table, digest agreement, caveats; `--compare-results DIR --compare-label NAME` adds before/after ratio tables. A route's own `kind` wins over `would_use_dense`; a `Batch route arms:` caption appears only when a batch arm is recorded. `route_text` reads a fused record as `fused/<backend> → fused` and appends `declined` when the record's `would_use_fused` is false.
- `check.py` — stdlib-only gate over ONE campaign result directory (`<dir>/<cell>/<arm>.json`): record presence/schema, `len(samples_ms) == reps >= 5`, medians re-derived from samples, `first_call_ms > 0`, evoxc route + compile-miss fields, `check_batch` (FAIL on unsupported mode / non-`batch` route / non-boolean `available`; WARN when the compile is attributed to no counter), `check_fused` (via `is_fused_record(arm, record)`, i.e. a registry-fused arm or a record whose RESOLVED engine — `resolved_engine(record)`, the first non-null `routes[*].engine` — is `fused`, so a record whose `eval_mode` is `auto` is gated as a fused measurement and a silent fallback cannot hide inside it: the routing FAIL accepts `eval_mode == "fused"` or `auto` with `engine == "fused"` and rejects a malformed `fused_cache` block, while a record that carries no `engine`/`fused_cache` or no probe of a launch is a WARN), `check_stages`/`check_content` (WARN-only, silent without those keys), cross-arm digest agreement, `gp_step` sanity, guard honesty, plus `--allow-snapshot`, `--allow-missing` (the 10 unscheduled pop ≥ 256 `evoxc-specialized-iree` cells) and `--allow-digest-mismatch` (the numpy-vs-iree and formation sha256 splits); exits 1 on failure.
  `gate_targets` gates a CONTAINER of campaigns campaign-by-campaign: when the target's children are result directories that each hold a `campaign.json` (e.g. `results/t1a1_fused/`, whose `main/` coexists with `cold/`, `probes/` and `sweep/`), it returns the children instead of merging every column into one giant pseudo-cell.
- `cold_start.py` — the cold-start column: ONE cell and ONE population per process from module import to the first synchronized `Forest.SR_fitness` on the DEFAULT route (`import_ms`/`workload_build_ms`/`first_call_ms`, cache misses, `--repeat`), with `--artifact-dir` running the disk layer as a populate child then a reload child.
- `training_run.py` — the whole-training-run column: one complete `StandardPipeline.step()` loop on the in-process `sr_test` problem (no dataset, no network) for `--arm engine|torch-per-tree|preopt`, per-generation sync-bracketed wall ms plus the best-fitness trajectory, `best_fitness_at`, trajectory digest and `final_pop_size`.
- `batch_economics.py` — the batch route's compile-economics column (stdlib driver, one child per `(cell, route)`; internals in `econ_child.py`): cold call → warm medians → a real `--generations 8` `StandardPipeline.step()` loop recording each generation's `structure_signature`, `signature_seen_before`, total/eval/ops ms, both caches' misses and `compile_hit`, plus the derived `summary`, `content` and `common.band_report` blocks.
  Default cells `p256-L32-i8-N1024,p1000-L32-i8-N1024,p1000-L64-i32-N1024`, routes the `ROUTE_SPECS` table (`batch`, `batch-flat`, `dense`, `batch-banded`, `batch-flat-chunked`, `batch-auto-chunked`, `batch-b16-banded`, `batch-b16-chunked`), default `--out-dir` `results/batch`; `--knob NAME=VALUE` layers a sweep over the spec and `protocol.route_knobs` records which layer won; `--evoxc-root` defaults to `/root/evoxc`, `EVOXC_DISABLE_LOCATIONS=1` is applied and recorded, `--dry-run` is torch-free, and `results/final|baseline|batch_opt_round2` are refused as `--out-dir`.
- `econ_child.py` — the child half: `CounterSurfaces`, `generation_entry`, `merged_cache_block`, `band_reports`, `generation_summary`, `measure`; torch-free at import.
- `reuse_probe.py` — the plan-key reuse workload, one child per column: a fixed four-call sequence on `p64-L64-i32-N64` (A cold, A identity, B rebuilt-but-equal verified by `forests.same_content`, B identity) with per-call wall time, digest, both counter surfaces and the ten stage CALL counts, plus a `verification` block whose expectations are `true`/`false`/`null`. `--out-dir` defaults to `results/batch_opt_round1/reuse`, `--dry-run` prints the plan torch-free.

## Constraints
- One process per timed config (the engine's compile cache, executable memo and input memo are per-process) and never two timed configs concurrently; never run anything else that imports torch while a campaign is in flight — the box has one usable device and the numbers are the deliverable.
- Cold compile never sits in the warm numbers: `worker.py` times the first call separately (`first_call_ms`, `cache.compile_misses_first_call`) before the warmup loop. A batch arm's large cold call is the measurement, not a defect; `check.py` has no upper bound on it and only WARNs when the compile is attributed to no counter.
- A timed workload needs `--reps >= 5` (`check.py` enforces `len(samples_ms) == reps >= MIN_SAMPLES`), so a `--reps 3` campaign is a pilot, not evidence. The round-2 protocol is `--warmup 2 --reps 5 --gp-warmup 1 --gp-reps 3 --seed 0 --workloads sr_fitness,forward,batch_forward` with a 900 s per-config cap, so one record holds three medians and pays three cold calls.
- Records are written verbatim: each JSON carries `evogp_commit`, `evogp_file`, `etl_version`, `env`, `gpu`, the guard verdict and its own protocol; a contaminated or re-run config is annotated, never smoothed.
- Warm medians are ±10 % on this shared box and a contended window inflates them 2–3×; read cold/first-call numbers as "± a factor of a few", and compare a knob only inside its own cell and window.
- `auto` resolves in this order: the fused (Triton) route whenever triton is importable, a torch device is usable and `pop_size >= EVOGP_FUSED_MIN_POP` (16); otherwise dense+iree from `pop >= EVOGP_DENSE_AUTO_MIN_POP` (64) on a device box; otherwise specialized+numpy (below that pop, on a CPU box or on any fallback). The dense leg's rows rule is a MEMORY ENVELOPE (`EVOGP_DENSE_AUTO_BUDGET_MB`, 8192; `EVOGP_DENSE_AUTO_MAX_ROWS` 0 = off), and it raises the dense trace cap to `_next_power_of_two(max(base, longest))` capped by `EVOGP_DENSE_AUTO_MAX_TREE_LEN` (256), only for `auto` and never when the cap is pinned — so an `evoxc-auto` median can be fused, dense-at-a-raised-cap or specialized, which is why a record states its resolved `engine` and its `auto_trace_cap`.
- The dense route's trace cap is `EVOGP_DENSE_MAX_TREE_LEN` (default 64): above `min(cap, forest.max_tree_len)` the dense evaluator raises `DenseUnsupported` and the public API silently falls back to the per-tree `specialized` route. `evoxc-dense-iree` measures that fallback, `evoxc-dense-iree-raised` pins the cap to the cell's `{tree_len}` and measures dense itself, and the record's `dense_route` says which happened. The cap is also a COST knob (2.03 → 5.28 → 19.50 ms at 31-node trees for cap 64 → 128 → 256), so raise it only to the depth actually generated.
- Tree length has two axes on the long-tree cells: the actual node count is `max_layer_cnt`-driven (seed 0 / pop 64: mean/max 15.8/29 at `d=5`, 27.1/57 at `d=6`, 35.5/101 at `d=7`, 69.9/187 at `d=8`), while the dense trace length is `tree_len`-driven, so the `-d<k>` sibling cells separate the two.
- `EVOGP_EVAL_MODE=batch` is the third route (`evogp/tree/batch_engine.py`): ONE merged graph per population structure multiset built on `evoxc.batch_functions`, iree with a numpy fallback, and `auto` never resolves to it. Knobs are single-sourced in `common.BATCH_KNOBS`: `EVOGP_BATCH_BAND_SIZE` 16, `EVOGP_BATCH_BANDS` (an explicit `K`, `none`/`flat`, or the opt-in `auto`), `EVOGP_BATCH_MIN_POP` 64, `EVOGP_BATCH_COMPILE_WORKERS` 4 (chunked concurrent compile; `0`/`1` = the single-executable path), `EVOGP_BATCH_CHUNK_BANDS` (off; the only way to chunk an explicit band spelling), `EVOGP_BATCH_SWITCH_OPS`, `EVOGP_BATCH_ALIGNMENT`, `EVOGP_BATCH_CACHE_SIZE`, and `EVOGP_BATCH_DISPATCH_FORMATION` (`auto` by default ⇒ `aggressive` only for an iree backend whose compile target is a GPU and whose evoxc adapter declares the option, otherwise `default`; the EFFECTIVE value lands on the plan and in every memo/executable/artifact key and is recorded as `band_report.dispatch_formation` next to the request).
- The batch route's persistent disk tier is `EVOGP_EVOXC_ARTIFACT_DIR` — ONE variable shared with the dense route, off when unset — with its own counters (`batch_artifact_stats()`); a disk hit skips `lower`/`compile`/`load`, so it is the only lever that moves the COLD column without touching the warm call. The key includes the chunk partition (pool width) and the effective formation, so a pool-4 directory does not serve a pool-1 or pool-8 run.
- A banded merge is capped by the device: one exported dispatch per band and 16 bindings per ROCm-HIP dispatch ⇒ `K <= 15` bands with `>= 8` merged functions per band (llvm-cpu `K <= 31`, numpy uncapped). The band-size model and `auto` are clamped and record `band_clamped`/`band_note`; an explicit illegal `EVOGP_BATCH_BANDS=K` raises `ValueError` naming the cap.
- Chunking is inert below 32 deduped functions (`MIN_CHUNK_FUNCTIONS`), so a pop-64 plan compiles as one body; with the shipped defaults (band size 16, pool 4, `CHUNK_BANDS` off) a pop-256 plan is a 4-chunk plan, i.e. the shipped default's measured column IS the `b16-cw4` composition while the `EVOGP_BATCH_COMPILE_WORKERS=1` rows are its single-body control.
- The per-stage split is a second, optional counter contract (`batch_engine.batch_stage_stats()`/`batch_stage_reset()`); without the reset every block stays cumulative, which is why records read before → after deltas instead of per-call blocks. A cold-stage reading must use `batch_compile_wall_ms`, never the per-chunk timer sum divided by the pool width (that sum counts concurrent work).
- The band knob is swept by `band_sweep.py` (a band PARTITION config pins the pool to 1, so a difference between two of them is the partition alone; a `*-cw4` config answers the banded+chunked question). A pure pool-width sweep is `batch_economics.py --knob EVOGP_BATCH_COMPILE_WORKERS=N` or a pool-pin arm.
- `specialized`-iree costs ~272–284 ms of compile per distinct tree structure, so that arm is scoped to pop ∈ {16, 64}; the dropped cells are listed in `campaign.json` (`protocol.scope_dropped`) instead of being silently omitted.
- `EVOGP_EVAL_MODE=fused` is the fourth route (`evogp/tree/fused_engine.py`, Triton kernel, no evoxc graph and no compile), selectable explicitly and — since `auto` reaches it first — also the shipped default's route on a device box; its knobs are single-sourced in `common.FUSED_ENV_VARS` (`EVOGP_FUSED_MIN_POP`, `EVOGP_FUSED_BLOCK_ROWS`, `EVOGP_FUSED_NUM_WARPS`, `EVOGP_FUSED_MAX_SCRATCH_MB`, `EVOGP_FUSED_HOST_MEMO`) and `auto`'s pop/rows envelope is the one `dense_engine.resolve_route` decision the record echoes. It needs a writable `TRITON_CACHE_DIR` (this box's `$HOME` is read-only), and a route that declines is indistinguishable from a served call by timing alone — read `cache.fused_served`, never the wall time.
- `before` carries no torch arms by design (the baselines are tree-root-independent, so the after values serve both sides).
- The project pytest gate (`pytest test src/evogp/tests`) DOES collect `test_large_cells.py` and `test_arm_registry.py` from this node (44 passed for the harness half); everything else here is hand-run.
- Those two modules run AFTER `src/evogp/tests` in that gate, whose `conftest.py` pins `EVOGP_EVOXC_BACKEND=numpy` for the session and RESTORES the caller's value (or its absence) at teardown, so a later-collected harness test sees no phantom pin and may inherit neither that environment nor a cached engine handle: every runtime engine lookup goes through `common.installed_module(...)` (a fresh `sys.modules` read, never `from evogp.tree import dense_engine`, which answers the parent package's cached attribute), and `test_arm_registry.py`'s `fake_dense_engine` clears `EVOGP_EVAL_MODE` / `EVOGP_EVOXC_BACKEND` / `common.ROUTE_ENV_VARS` before it decides. Both collection orders (`pytest src/evogp/tests test/benchmark/evoxc -q` and its reverse) read 1 352 passed / 7 skipped / 0 failed, which is how an env-leak/order bug is caught.

## Adding a new arm (an additional evaluation route)
Registration is data, not code: an evoxc-style route is one entry in `common.EVOXC_ARMS` carrying `{"eval_mode", "backend", "family"}` plus an optional `"knobs"` mapping (env name → value, `None` = leave unset): the flat merge, the formation control, the cell-free `{artifact_dir}` of the artifact arm, a per-cell template such as `EVOGP_DENSE_MAX_TREE_LEN={tree_len}`, or a pool pin.
`common.ALL_ARMS` is derived, so `--arms all|evoxc|batch` and `check.py`'s coverage pick it up with no further edit; append new arms LAST, because registry order is the render order (`summarize.order_arms`) and the `auto route` column's fallback.
`arms.apply_arm_env(arm, cell=None)` writes the route selectors and then the spec's `knobs`, resolved through `arms.resolve_knob_value` against the cell dict, after clearing every `common.ROUTE_ENV_VARS` entry (it `KeyError`s on an unregistered name).
Arm methods take no arguments (`getattr(arm_obj, name)()`) and the object exposes `setup_ms`, `family` and `fitness(forest, report=None)`; a route needing a different call path needs its own class in `arms.py` (the batch arms do not — they use the public `Forest.SR_fitness`/`batch_forward`/`forward`).
A new route's counters must be pointed at their own stats callable (null, never 0, when missing): compile economics come from `evoxc_engine.cache_stats()`, batch counters from `evogp.tree.batch_engine.batch_cache_stats`, the per-stage split is registered in `stages.py`, the disk tier in `artifact.py`, the plan key in `content.py` — and it should record its own resolved policy block the way a batch arm records `batch_policy` (`check_batch` is the model for its gate: routing as FAIL, counter attribution as WARN).
`check.py` treats any arm absent from `TORCH_ARMS` as an evoxc arm, so it must record `routes` plus a non-`None` `cache.compile_misses_first_call`, and its `batch_forward`/`forward` digests and `nan`/`finite` counts must match the other arms of the cell (or be declared with `--allow-digest-mismatch`). `campaign.SCOPE` is the per-arm coverage rule (it scopes `evoxc-specialized-iree`, and the batch arms have none) and `campaign.CELL_EXCLUSIONS` the per-`(cell, arm)` one.
A record is gated as a fused record by its RESOLVED engine (`is_fused_record`), not by its arm's registry entry, so an arm that only differences its env can reach the fused route without a registry entry of its own — and then has to carry the launch proof (`fused_cache`, a positive `fused_kernel_launches` or a cold-cache `fused_kernel_compiles`) like any explicit fused arm.
A route that only differences its ENV needs no registry arm at all: it is one `ROUTE_SPECS` entry in `batch_economics.py` (validated against `common.ROUTE_ENV_VARS`; `--knob` overrides it), and `protocol.route_knobs` states which layer won.
`summarize.py` keeps its own `_BATCH_KNOB_ORDER` for the caption (an unknown knob is simply not rendered), derives the route kind from the record's own `routes[...]kind`, and sources the `auto route` column from `evoxc-auto`/`evoxc-auto-iree` first.
`cold_start.py`, `training_run.py`, `decompose.py` and `batch_economics.py` keep their OWN route tables and do NOT read the arm registry, so a new route needs a separate edit in each to be measured there.

## Known Issues
- `EVOGP_BATCH_BANDS=auto` is neither accurate nor stable and stays opt-in: ROCm/iree binds `{max_bands 15, min_band_size 8}`, the static cost model overshoots it 14.2–14.7× on the pop-256 loss plans (`band_requested_bands` 213/220 → `bands` 15, `band_clamped true`), undershoots to 4 bands on the per-row `forward` plan and to a bare 1 (the flat merge) at pop 64, is not population-stable (one `population_signature` reads K = 213 in its sweep and K = 1 in a loop's first call), lost all 9 warm columns of its grid, and its clamp costs +0.18–1.11 s of cold host merge per call. Evidence `results/batch_opt_round2/{auto,composition,smoke-cold}/`.
- Flat (`EVOGP_BATCH_BANDS=none`) does NOT chunk even at pool 4 (`compile_workers 1`/`chunk_count 1`), because the chunk gate refuses an explicit band spelling unless `EVOGP_BATCH_CHUNK_BANDS` opts in; a band SIZE does not refuse chunking.
- Banded+chunked is a COLD lever only: 2.64×/2.94× cheaper cold at the two pop-256 cells (10.73/11.96 s → 4.06/4.07 s) for 1.69–1.87× the warm call (break-even 3 849 / 5 103 warm calls of one population, 18 702 at 111-node trees); inert at `p64`.
- A generation loop never amortizes the merge: every generation presents a new structure multiset (7 of 8 compile, `generation_hit_rate` 0.125), so the batch route is 17.8–71.5× behind dense over an 8-generation loop at pop 256 and 68–178× at large scale. Compile-pool width for a compile-dominated loop is still OPEN (no loop arms above pool 4; pool 8 is cheaper cold and dearer warm, §7.1 of `LARGE_SCALE_ROUND2.md` records the break-evens).
- `EVOGP_BATCH_DISPATCH_FORMATION=aggressive` is a partial win, not the 7–9 % its owner predicted: −4.4/−1.1/+1.6 % on `sr_fitness` in the 3-cell A/B and −4.9/−4.8/−0.1/−1.5/−0.2/+1.9/−14.6/−5.9 % across the round-2 cells (cold −11.3/−19.3/−0.6/−2.0/−6.1/−3.5/−36.5/−17.4 %), ~−6 to −7 % on `forward` at pop 2 048/1 000, and a sign flip inside noise on the smallest plan; `emitted_ops` are IDENTICAL between the two spellings (the fusion happens inside iree, so no harness-visible dispatch count) and numerics are not bitwise — 1 of 256 elements differs, 4.24e-05 relative on `sr_fitness`, 8.81e-08 on `forward`, reproducible and attributable (a same-formation control is bitwise exact). It needs an evoxc that DECLARES the option; without one it degrades to `default`. Evidence `results/largascale2/ab_dispatch/`. The per-cell delta is window-sensitive and only its sign and the ordering are robust: the re-measured `p2048-L64-i32-N1024` window reads −11.4 % (12.41 against a 14.00 ms control) instead of −4.9 %, the control arm's own cold spread being 50.7 s against 43.8 s.
- The artifact tier turns a cold call into 4.10 s (from 44.27 s, 10.79×) at pop 2 048 and 2.21 s (from 16.98 s, 7.68×) at pop 1 000 / `N` 4 096 with zero compiles on the hit run and identical digests, but a hit still pays 82–86 % of the call in host decode/dedup/trace/merge; the artifact LOAD is not stage-instrumented (load calls 0, no `batch_load_total_ms`), so a hit's disk time is invisible and folded into the residual. No byte-size counter exists, so file sizes are not record-backed.
- `torch-batched` wins the single-call column outright at large scale against every compiled evoxc route (8 of 9 cells, up to 7.46× over dense and 0.42–0.58× of the batch route at pop 2 048–8 192), and only a fused device-side evaluator changes that — the r6 Triton prototype (`../r6/SUMMARY.md`) is the measured evidence that it can, and the shipped `fused` route is it (`auto` resolves it on a device box, measured in `results/t1a1_auto_r3/`).
- The merged-graph compile is roughly quadratic in the plan's emitted ops and rows-insensitive (`48.9 s × (ops/41 376)²`, within ~10 %): 42 s at pop 2 048, 153 s at 4 096, 800 s (782.5 s of compile, 89 % of the 900 s cap) at pop 8 192 with 6 541 functions / 152 143 ops. Pop 8 192 is feasible; 187-node trees × pop 2 048 (`d8`) are not; both are compile economics, not defects.
- Explicit `EVOGP_EVAL_MODE=dense` on `p2048-L128-i32-N1024-d7` OOM-kills the job unit (the default cap declines into per-tree `specialized`+iree with unbounded artifact growth); the d7 arms must be scheduled without `evoxc-dense-iree` and `check.py` reports it as a WARN (`missing arms (allowed)`).
- The long-tree `d7`/`d8` cells split into four sha256 groups per value workload and need `--allow-digest-mismatch`: `specialized`+iree contracts `mul` into the following `add`/`sub` (FMA), ≤2.2e-3 relative at d7 and ≤6.1e-3 at d8 after deep-tree cancellation (minimal repro `add(mul(x0,x1), x0)`), while dense differs from numpy only in the NaN payload bit on `sub`.
  On the re-measured `main/` column the split is per workload and `sr_fitness` is not sha-gated at all: `forward` carries three groups on both pop-256 cells, `p2048` and `d7` and four on the other five (each batch spelling its own), `batch_forward` two at the pop-256 cells, three at pop 1 000/`p4096-i64` and four at `p2048`/`p4096`/`d7`, i.e. the fusion moves low bits per workload and never the loss-path arithmetic identity.
- The batch `batch_forward` is non-bitwise on the `arith` set where dense is exact (1.5625e-02 abs / 1.13e-03 rel at `p256-L32-i8-N1024`), which is why the batch arm forms its own digest group; the loss and per-row paths agree to ~1e-7 relative.
- Asking for the batch route below the population floor costs ≈1.6 ms per call more than asking `specialized` directly (3.94 vs 2.31 ms at pop 16), so the decline path is not free.
- `check.py` cannot gate a `band_sweep.py` directory (band-sweep schema, no `campaign.json`) or a `batch_economics` directory (its `economics.json` fails the arm-record schema) — a sweep's gate is its own `sweep.json`, and an economics round's gate unit is a campaign-format subdirectory (`results/batch_opt_round1/campaign`).
- `batch_economics.py` has no idle-device guard: its `economics.json` carries no `guard` block, so a loop run's idleness must be verified by hand with `rocm-smi` and the runs kept strictly sequential.
- `validate.py` has no named batch entry — a batch sweep is produced by exporting the route, not by a set in the tool.
- `reuse_probe.py`'s plan-key win is the rebuilt-but-equal-population regime only (2.724 ms against the pre-round 79.109 ms at `p64-L64-i32-N64`); in a loop it buys nothing (6 attempts / 0 hits). `BATCH.md` §13.10.
- The committed `results/batch/**` records predate the per-stage split and the plan fields (`compile_workers`/`chunk_count`/`chunk_sizes`), so later rounds re-measure those cells instead of back-filling them.
- The guard's `python` hint field lists the harness's own `systemd-run` launcher chain, not a foreign tenant; `contaminated` is false and every campaign of record reports `status ok` with `idle` verdicts.
- Coverage limits: `evoxc-specialized-iree` is measured on pop ∈ {16, 64} only; `validate.py`'s `rich`-set spread is the route's documented approximation, not a regression.
- The `etl` names inside the R1–R6 records, payload keys and logs are the pre-rename name of `evoxc`; the committed records are quoted verbatim and never re-keyed.

- The fused route is registered as a first-class arm pair (`evoxc-fused-iree`, `evoxc-fused`, appended LAST in `common.EVOXC_ARMS`) and the gate proves a launch rather than a silent fallback: a `FusedUnsupported` falls back to the route's `fallback_backend` (specialized+numpy with no backend pinned, specialized+**iree** whenever `EVOGP_EVOXC_BACKEND` is set) and the route compiles no evoxc graph, so `cache.compile_misses_first_call` is 0 by construction — the record's `cache.fused_served` (a `fused_kernel_launches`/`fused_kernel_compiles` delta from `fused_engine.fused_cache_stats()`, 15 keys, separate from the pinned 8-key `evoxc_engine.cache_stats()`) is the only existence proof, and it reads `null` rather than `false` when the counters are absent.
  `cold_start.py` still hardcodes `apply_arm_env("evoxc-auto")` (the default route) and `decompose.py`'s `ROUTES` table has no fused entry, so a cold-start or per-phase fused column needs an edit in each; the route's own dev battery is `../../src/evogp/tree/fused_check.py` (parity/goldens/declines/wired/timing/tuning, importing `validate.compare` and `common` from here), whose `--stage all` run reads PARITY 56/56, MULTI 65/65, GOLDENS 10/10, DECLINES 57/57, WIRED 32/32, TIMING worst 102.59× `torch-batched` and TUNING minimax = the shipped default (block rows 256 / 1 warp).
  The six-cell campaign in `results/t1a1_fused/` predates the counter surface, so its fused records still gate with WARNs (`routes.sr_fitness carries no 'engine'`, `no fused_cache block`) instead of a launch proof.
  `results/t1a1_auto_r3/` (and its pre-change baseline `results/t1a1_auto/`) is the directory that DOES prove the launch (24 `fused_kernel_launches`, 21 of them bound launches, 0 declines, `served_every_workload true` on all six cells), and both scheduled `forward` on top of `sr_fitness`/`batch_forward` because a `--workloads sr_fitness,batch_forward`-scoped campaign reports the declared `no digest anywhere in the cell` class for the value workload it never ran.
