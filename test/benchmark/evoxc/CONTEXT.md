# `test/benchmark/evoxc/` — evoxc engine vs plain-PyTorch controlled harness

## Intent
Answer one question with evidence: what does the evoxc-based EvoGP engine cost per evaluation compared with the plain-PyTorch implementation of the same tree evaluation, on identical populations, in the same session, warm/steady-state.
The final campaign widens that question to the post-optimization tree: HEAD against the pre-optimization snapshot `530518e` measured in one window, plus a cold-start column and whole-training-run columns; `FINAL.md` is the report of record.
It is the "new engine vs old torch version" counterpart to `../r4/`/`../r5/`/`../r6/`, which compare the evoxc engine against the deleted legacy CUDA engine; those rounds are frozen history and are never edited from here.
Every arm is validated numerically against `src/evogp/tests/reference_eval.py` before its timings are trusted (`VALIDATION.md`), and every record states the environment, the route that actually resolved, and the cold compile it paid.

## Contents
- `common.py` — ONE definition of every workload: the cell grid (`main_cells()`/`n64_cells()`/`parse_cell`), deterministic `build_workload` (`torch.manual_seed(0)` + `Forest.random_generate`), the `+ - * /` descriptor, `env_block` (git SHA, versions, GPU, knobs), `time_call` (cold call separated from warmup+reps medians), `digest`, `route_decision` (asks the policy's own `resolve_eval_mode`/`resolve_backend_name`/`should_use_dense`, so a record reports the real decision instead of a re-derivation), `src_root()`/`checkout_root()`.
- `arms_torch.py` — the two pure-torch baselines: `PerTreeEvaluator` (Python tree walk, torch ops per node) and `BatchedEvaluator` (one vectorized pass over the population, per-position masked `torch.where`, per-row child gather). Op semantics are copied from the oracle.
- `arms.py` — the arm namespace: `TorchArm`/`EvoxcArm`, `apply_arm_env` (`EVOGP_EVAL_MODE`/`EVOGP_EVOXC_BACKEND` per arm), `make_arm`, `SRLossProblem` + `make_pipeline` for the GP generation step; `evoxc-auto` leaves both knobs unset so it times the shipped policy itself, and `evoxc-auto-iree` only differs from it below the dense threshold (specialized+iree instead of specialized+numpy).
- `worker.py` — ONE process, ONE `(cell, arm)`: cold call, warmup, reps, cache deltas, value digests, `gp_step` split into eval/operator, per-workload `first_call_ms`. Honors `--tree-root`.
- `campaign.py` — the sequential driver: one subprocess per config, idle-device guard (`rocm-smi --showpids`, excluding the driver's own ancestor chain), `--tree-root`/`--resume`/scope policy, per-config `.log` + `campaign.json` with the guard verdict per config.
- `validate.py` — every arm vs the oracle and vs the engine, three function sets (`arith` bitwise-mandatory, `rich` transcendental spread, `ternary` for the `if` operand order), 12 cases over the four scheduled cells.
- `decompose.py` — per-call phase breakdown of one cell on both routes (inclusive `perf_counter_ns` timers on the engine's own functions, residual row for host glue, seam counters for dispatches/uploads/readbacks, cold compile separate).
- `summarize.py` — stdlib-only renderer: environment block, per-workload tables (median with min in parentheses), `auto` route per cell, speedup vs both torch arms, the `gp_step` section (total, eval/ops split with the operator share, speedup vs both torch arms, `best_fitness`), cold-compile table, digest agreement, caveats; `gp_step` cells fall back to the upper median of `steps[].total_ms` / `steps[].eval_ms` (marked `*`) when a record lacks the aggregate keys, and `--compare-results DIR --compare-label NAME` adds one before/after ratio table per workload plus one for the `gp_step` total.
- `check.py` — stdlib-only gate over ONE campaign result directory (`<dir>/<cell>/<arm>.json`): record presence/schema, `len(samples_ms) == reps >= 5`, medians re-derived from samples and `gp_step`'s `median_total_ms` from `steps[].total_ms`, `first_call_ms > 0`, evoxc route + compile-miss fields, cross-arm digest agreement, `gp_step` sanity, guard honesty, plus `--allow-snapshot` (a null `env.tree_root_sha` warns instead of failing when the recorded tree root has no `.git`), `--allow-missing` (the 10 unscheduled pop ≥ 256 `evoxc-specialized-iree` cells) and `--allow-digest-mismatch` (the numpy-vs-iree sha256 split); exits 1 on failure.
- `cold_start.py` — the cold-start column: ONE cell and ONE population per process (the in-memory compile cache is per process) from module import to the first synchronized `Forest.SR_fitness` value on the DEFAULT route, with `import_ms`/`workload_build_ms`/`first_call_ms`, `cache_stats()` compile misses before/after the first call and `--repeat` warm-cache repeats; `--cell`, `--pop` (replaces a loop: one process per population), `--tree-root`, `--artifact-dir` drives the opt-in disk layer (`EVOGP_EVOXC_ARTIFACT_DIR`) as two child processes (populate, then reload), `--phase populate|reload` is the internal half selector those children run as, and a tree without `artifact_cache.py` records `artifact_cache: "absent"`.
- `training_run.py` — the end-to-end training-run column: one complete `StandardPipeline`/`GeneticProgramming` run on the in-process `sr_test` symbolic-regression problem (no dataset, no network) with `--arm engine|torch-per-tree|preopt`, `--pop`/`--generations`/`--seed`/`--tree-len`/`--input-len`/`--num-data`/`--report-gens`, per-generation sync-bracketed wall ms plus the full best-fitness trajectory, `best_fitness_at` (gens 10/25/50), the trajectory digest and `final_pop_size` — driven through `pipeline.step()`, i.e. the library's own loop, so the fitness callable is called exactly as the library calls it.
- `results/` — the evidence: `baseline/` (the earlier round's committed records, frozen and untouched, rendered by `SUMMARY.md`), `final/after/` (130 records at HEAD: the two torch arms plus `evoxc-auto`, `evoxc-auto-iree`, `evoxc-dense-iree`, `evoxc-specialized-numpy` and `evoxc-specialized-iree` on the pop ∈ {16, 64} cells only), `final/before/` (90 records, the same harness against the snapshot `/tmp/evogp_preopt` = commit `530518e` via `--tree-root`, evoxc arms only), `final/{decompose,decompose-before}/` and `final/{cold,training}/` (one-cell / one-run records, their own schema), `final/validation.json` (the 12-case sweep) and `final/RENDERED.md` (the three `summarize.py` renders as plain text).
- `FINAL.md` — the report of record for the post-optimization campaign: the arm redefinition, the headline/compare/decomposition tables, cold-start economics, the 50- and 200-generation training runs, numeric validation, the noise audit, the gate and the reproduce block.
- `SUMMARY.md` — the rendered report of the earlier baseline round (`summarize.py --results results/baseline`), kept as that round's record; its header states that the current numbers live in `FINAL.md` and that its `evoxc-auto` column is the specialized route (the baseline policy never selected dense).
- `VALIDATION.md` — numeric validation of the final sweep plus the cross-arm digest-agreement finding and its practical relevance.
- `README.md` — methodology, arms, grid, the final-campaign section (what was measured, the exact commands, the one-line verdicts), report/gate rules, validation and decomposition notes.

## Constraints
- One process per timed config (the engine's compile cache, executable memo and input memo are per-process), and never two timed configs concurrently.
- `cold_start.py` measures one population per process for the same reason: a `pop` loop inside one process would be cold only for its first population (use `--pop` and one process per value).
- `training_run.py` drives `StandardPipeline.step()` instead of `run()` (which is the same loop plus the stopping rules) so each generation is a timed unit; the fitness callable still goes through `problem.evaluate`. Arms share one seed and one problem construction, and speed is only read next to `best_fitness_by_generation`.
- `auto` resolves to dense+iree at `pop >= 64` on a device box and to specialized+numpy below that, on a CPU box, or on any fallback (`EVOGP_DENSE_AUTO_MIN_POP` moves the threshold, `EVOGP_DENSE_AUTO_MAX_ROWS` the rows ceiling), so the `evoxc-auto` arm measures the dense path at pop ≥ 64 and the specialized path at pop 16.
- `before` carries no torch arms by design: the torch baselines are tree-root-independent, so their after values are the reference for both sides.
- The gate of record is per side — `python3 check.py --results results/final/after --allow-snapshot --allow-missing --allow-digest-mismatch` (130 records / 3602 checks) or `--results results/final/before` (90 records / 2642 checks) — because `results/final/cold/` and `results/final/training/` hold a different schema so the container directory is not gateable in one run, and because the 10 unscheduled `evoxc-specialized-iree` cells and the numpy-vs-iree digest split are declared expectations rather than defects.
- Never run anything else that imports torch while a campaign is in flight — the box has one usable device and the numbers are the deliverable.
- Cold compile never sits in the warm numbers: `worker.py` times the first call separately (`first_call_ms`, `cache.compile_misses_first_call`) before the warmup loop.
- `specialized`-iree costs ~272-284 ms of compile per distinct tree structure on this box, so that arm is scoped to `pop ∈ {16, 64}`; the dropped cells are listed in `campaign.json` (`protocol.scope_dropped`) instead of being silently omitted.
- Records are written verbatim: a contaminated or re-run cell is annotated (`--note`, guard block), never smoothed.
- `results/baseline/` and `results/final/**` are frozen evidence; a re-run writes into a fresh result directory, and `../r2/`, `../r4/`, `../r5/`, `../r6/` are never touched from here.
- This subtree is not collected by pytest and is not part of the `src/evogp/tests/` suite.

## Known Issues
- The guard's `python` hint field lists the harness's own `systemd-run` launcher chain, not a foreign tenant; `contaminated` stays false and the gate reads `ok`/`idle` on all 220 records (130/130 after, 90/90 before), with empty `gpu_pids`.
- Run without the three allow flags, the gate reports the declared expectations as failures: 38 on `results/final/after` and 39 on `results/baseline` (the 10 unscheduled `evoxc-specialized-iree` cells plus the numpy-vs-iree sha256 split on `forward`/`batch_forward`); pointing it at the container `results/final` fails on the `cold/`, `training/` and `decompose*/` subdirectories' different schema.
- `../r5/probe_lib.py` still proxies `getattr(dense_module, "etl")` and raises on the current engine, so the r5/r6 probe measurements are historical while their stdlib gates over the committed records still run.
- The `etl`-shadow halves of R2/R3/R4 remain non-re-runnable (their session-scoped shadow exports are gone); the committed records and per-log probe lines are the surviving evidence.
- Coverage limits: `evoxc-specialized-iree` is measured on pop ∈ {16, 64} only (272-284 ms of compile per tree structure makes a dense-scale cell unaffordable), and no `CustomLoss`/multi-output or robotics workload is validated or timed.

## Routing Table
- `FINAL.md` → the report of record for the post-optimization campaign; its §11 holds the exact reproduce commands and §12 the caveats worth carrying forward.
- `SUMMARY.md` → the rendered baseline round (protocol statement, per-workload tables, `gp_step`, cold compile, digest agreement, caveats) with a header pointing at `FINAL.md`.
- `VALIDATION.md` → the 12-case oracle validation and the cross-arm digest-group finding.
- `README.md` → the entry point: the question, the arms, the grid/protocol, the final-campaign section (measured grid, commands, verdicts), the report/gate rules and the rules for this subtree.
- `results/` → data only (`baseline/` and `final/`, the latter with `RENDERED.md`); the reports of record are `FINAL.md` and `VALIDATION.md` at this node.
- `../r4/`, `../r5/`, `../r6/` → the historical legacy-CUDA-engine comparison; read-only from here.
- `../../../src/evogp/tests/reference_eval.py` → the oracle every arm is validated against.
